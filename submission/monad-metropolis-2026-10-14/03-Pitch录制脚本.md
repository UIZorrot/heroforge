# Pitch：建议 1 分 40 秒，必须小于 2 分钟

这是可录制的口播稿，不是视频成品。先把 `[NAME]` 替换为实际出镜者姓名；如为团队，增加一句真实成员与分工介绍。不要填写未经确认的履历。

## 英文口播

Hi, I'm [NAME], and I'm building HeroForge with our team.

HeroForge starts with a simple creative idea: an image can become a hero with a name, a story, and a place in a tactical team. We want players to care about characters they helped create, and to experiment with team composition instead of collecting only predefined characters.

The game combines AI-assisted character concepts with rule-based combat stats. Our training direction lets players test their own teams without ranked stakes, while our matchmaking direction keeps comparable battle power in mind.

For this hackathon, we have shipped a focused collectible prototype on Monad Testnet. Players can open our public page, connect a wallet, choose a hero portrait or provide a public image URL, and mint an ERC-721 card through a real transaction. The contract is deployed and source-verified, and the page reads ownership and metadata from the chain.

We chose Monad because it gives us an EVM environment for building this layer with familiar wallets and contract tools. Today, combat remains offchain. Our next step is to explore how collectible ownership can connect to the game without undermining balance or adding friction to experimentation.

We plan to start with tactical-card players, creators, and Monad playtesters, measuring repeat use and successful mint flows before expanding. You can try the live prototype now at hf.txzy.net slash monad. Thank you.
