## Project name

HeroForge

## Track

Social, Attention & Culture

## One-line description

HeroForge turns images into tactical hero cards, with a live Monad Testnet prototype for minting collectible hero snapshots to a player's wallet.

## Long description

HeroForge is a tactical hero-card game built around player-created characters. Its broader game loop uses images and AI-assisted character concepts to create heroes, with a rule-based numeric engine defining combat stats. Players can experiment with their own teams in training and pursue tactical team combat. The goal is to make creativity and team composition part of the game, rather than relying only on a fixed catalogue of characters.

Our live hackathon deliverable adds an ERC-721 collectible layer on Monad Testnet. At https://hf.txzy.net/monad/, players can connect an EVM wallet, choose one of four illustrated heroes or provide a public HTTPS/IPFS portrait URL, enter a name and battle-power snapshot, and mint a card through a real Monad transaction. The page reads the wallet's token ownership and metadata directly from the deployed contract. Names, archetypes, battle-power snapshots and image references are recorded onchain; the JSON metadata is returned by the contract, while artwork remains publicly hosted.

The contract is deployed and source-verified on Monad Testnet, and real mint transactions are publicly inspectable. The mint page is available without a HeroForge email login. Our current game combat remains offchain: this release does not claim that NFT ownership authorizes battle stats, game-account ownership, or trading functionality.

Monad gives us an EVM environment in which we can build and verify this ownership layer using existing wallet and contract tooling. Future work will explore connecting collectible ownership to the broader game, while preserving its matchmaking and balance rules. Those integrations are future work, not functionality already delivered in this prototype.

## User acquisition strategy

We plan to start with small playtest groups drawn from tactical-card players, creative communities and Monad builders. The first call to action is concrete: create or choose a hero portrait, mint a collectible on the live testnet page, and share the resulting card and transaction.

Short creator-led demonstrations will show both the character fantasy and the actual mint flow. We will invite early users to report wallet friction, artwork issues and the appeal of returning to build more heroes. In parallel, playtests of the existing training and team-combat loop will help determine whether the collectible layer strengthens a game people want to keep playing.

We will measure successful mints, repeat use, creator sharing and participation in subsequent playtests before expanding distribution. Our next growth experiments are a small themed hero event and a feedback-driven creator cohort. These are proposed acquisition activities; we are not claiming existing traction, partnerships or user counts.
